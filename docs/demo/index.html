Pocket Harp Club

A friendly club for people learning the ten-hole diatonic harmonica. We meet on Thursday evenings and play everything from folk tunes to blues.

New here?

Start with Getting started. It covers choosing your first harmonica, how to hold it, and playing single notes. When single notes feel easy, move on to bending notes, which is where the harmonica starts to sound like a voice.

Club meetings

Meetings start with a warm-up tune everyone can play, followed by small groups by level. Bring a harmonica in the key of C if you have one. We keep a box of spare instruments for visitors.

Tunes we play

  • Oh Susanna - a first tune using holes 4 to 6
  • Amazing Grace - practice for smooth breathing
  • A slow twelve-bar blues in second position
  • Irish reels for the brave

Looking after your harmonica

A well-kept harmonica lasts for years. Tap it gently against your palm after playing, keep it in its case, and never share instruments without cleaning them. The tuning and care page has more detail.
